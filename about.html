ABOUT BICHI

Connecting people and local drivers.

BICHI is a ride-booking platform intended to make local journeys easier to arrange by connecting customers with drivers through a mobile application.

What we do

Customers can enter pickup and destination information, request a ride, view trip progress and review the fare shown for their journey. Drivers can receive and manage ride requests through the driver application.

Our approach

We aim to provide a straightforward experience with clear trip details, customer support and payment instructions presented through the app.

Service availability

Ride availability depends on location, driver availability, operating hours and other service conditions. The app will show the options available for a particular request.

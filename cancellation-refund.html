Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Ride cancellations

Customers may cancel a ride using the cancellation option in the app, subject to the cancellation terms shown at the time of booking. Any applicable cancellation fee must be clearly disclosed before it is charged.

2. Duplicate or failed payments

If an online payment appears to have been deducted more than once or a payment succeeds but the app does not update, contact BICHI support with the ride ID, transaction reference, amount and payment date. Do not share your UPI PIN, OTP or full card details.

3. Refund eligibility

Refund requests will be reviewed based on the ride status, payment confirmation, duplicate payment evidence and applicable law. Approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method where supported by the payment provider.

4. Refund timelines

After a refund is approved and initiated, the time taken for the funds to appear depends on the bank, UPI app and payment provider. BICHI will provide a reference where available.

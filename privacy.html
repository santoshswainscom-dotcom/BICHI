Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BICHI ("BICHI", "we", "us") handles personal information when people use the BICHI website and app.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, information may include name, phone number, account details, pickup and destination locations, ride history, device and app information, support messages and payment status or transaction references.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, arrange rides, support customers and drivers, provide trip updates, process or verify payments, prevent fraud, improve the service and meet legal obligations.

3. Location information

When permitted by your device settings, location information may be used to set pickup points, support navigation and provide ride-related location updates. You can manage device location permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

4. Sharing

Relevant trip details may be shared between a customer and the assigned driver to fulfil a ride. Information may also be shared with service providers such as hosting, maps, communications and payment providers, or with authorities where legally required.

5. Payments

Online payment details are handled by the payment provider. BICHI may receive payment status and transaction references. Do not send full card details, UPI PINs or passwords to BICHI support.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards and retain information for as long as needed for service delivery, dispute handling, security and legal requirements. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

To request access, correction or deletion where applicable, or to raise a privacy concern, contact santosh.swain.s.com@gmail.com.
